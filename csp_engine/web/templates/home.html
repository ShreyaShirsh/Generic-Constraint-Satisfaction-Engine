BITE308L · A Generic Constraint Satisfaction Engine

One engine. One constraint. Thirty problems.

Logic riddles, sliding puzzles, chess, Sudoku, routing, scheduling, robots and jigsaws all go through the same solver. Every problem below compiles to the same object, and the engine is built on exactly one kind of constraint:

constraint.is_satisfied(assignment) → True / False a yes/no predicate over a set of variables — and the one used most is ≠
Explore the problems Puzzle Studio How the engine works

Puzzles

Variables, domains and constraints, as in the textbook. Most constraints are ≠.

Search & planning

Variables are "the move at step t"; one predicate says the plan is legal and reaches the goal.

Optimisation

A normal CSP plus one predicate: "better than the best solution found so far".

Games

A quantified CSP: ∃ my move ∀ your reply ∃ … one WIN predicate.
