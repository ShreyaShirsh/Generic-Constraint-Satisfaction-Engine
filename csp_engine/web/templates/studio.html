Endless puzzles, graded by the AI

The engine grows a random solution, removes givens one by one while the solution stays unique (it re-solves after every removal to prove it), then grades difficulty by the hardest kind of reasoning a person would need: Easy = direct elimination, Medium = "only place left", Hard = arithmetic/inequality propagation, subsets and intersections, Expert = logic alone isn't enough, you'd have to guess.
